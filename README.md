**A Practical Guide from Loft Furniture & Fabric, Singapore**

A chair rarely fails all at once. First the fabric fades, then the seat flattens, then it ends up in a corner. The good news is that upholstery for chairs can bring it back to daily use with a fresh look and proper support.

**What Gets Replaced During Chair Upholstery**

A professional job goes deeper than swapping fabric. Loft Furniture & Fabric typically works through these stages:
• Removing the old fabric and checking the frame
• Replacing worn foam and padding
• Fitting new fabric with clean, even tension
• Final finishing and quality check

**Matching the Job to the Room**

**Dining Areas**
Seats here need durability. Dining seat upholstery with washable or stain resistant fabric handles spills and heavy use. Foam thickness also affects how comfortable long meals feel.
**Living Spaces**
Accent chairs and armchairs suit softer textures such as linen blends or velvet. This is where home furniture upholstery can tie your sofa, chairs, and cushions into one look.
**Work and Commercial Spaces**
Cafes and offices benefit from tough, wipe clean materials that keep a neat appearance over time.

**Questions to Ask Before You Book**

Q: How do I know if my chair is worth reupholstering?
A: If the frame is solid and does not wobble, it is usually a good candidate. Fabric and foam are the parts most often worn out.
Q: What should I look for in a provider?
A: Choose an upholstery service Singapore homeowners trust, with a clear process, fabric samples, and honest advice on whether repair or replacement makes more sense.
